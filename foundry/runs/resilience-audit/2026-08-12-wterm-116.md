# wterm PR #116 resilience audit

Date: 2026-08-12
Head: `4852dde5481439883eecf7f61f32f6091be5b468`
Verdict: **fail**

## Findings

1. High: frame-separated scrollback rollover double-adjusts the history position because wterm and Chromium both own anchoring. Before: `history 0354`, `scrollTop 6000`. After 40 discarded rows: `history 0314`, `scrollTop 4640`; expected 5320. `overflow-anchor: none` restored the exact oracle.
2. Medium: the built-in core silently degrades new OSC 8 links to text after 1024 host-lifetime identities.
3. Medium: the Ghostty adapter cache grew 800, 1024, 1200, and 1600 entries after the same number of identities were resolved. RIS did not prune it; a fresh core reset it.

## Preserved

- Real viewport resize bursts recovered the original row and offset after geometry stabilized.
- Pausing and resuming a stream before rollover preserved the anchor and bounded DOM.
- Mounted DOM rows remained bounded.

## Limits

Firefox, Safari, long-duration heap/RSS, browser-rendered Ghostty pressure, and transport reconnect were not executed.
