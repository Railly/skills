## Commit point and recovery

The durable commit point is the atomic rename of the owner-fixed temporary
token after the daemon owns the listener. Failed publication occurs before
that commit point and leaves the prior token intact. Immediate replacement
retry succeeds after the obstruction is removed. Shutdown and clean own final
and temporary artifact removal.

Concurrent publication is excluded by listener ownership: only the daemon that
successfully binds publishes. Repeated client requests reuse the live token and
fresh per-request challenges without growing retained state.
