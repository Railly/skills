Proposed shapes

Argument, not measurement. Neither proposer ran the code.

A — Claude Sonnet: format enum on the subscription

guessed Every service can carry a format at its registration site. The observed half shows two cannot.

inferred A fifth service still edits core, so the ownership property is half restored. Stated by the proposal itself.

observed Its stated risk, one dispatcher shared by unlike plugins, is impossible here. Probe P4.
