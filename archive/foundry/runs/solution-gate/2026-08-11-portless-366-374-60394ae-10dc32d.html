Chosen grafts

  • #366: Proposal B's parsed framework slice and insertion index, plus Proposal A's independent Expo host-mode suppression.
  • #374: Proposal B's producer-state preservation and test-harness correction, plus the shared matching hardening.
  • Do not shorten the production old-daemon fallback to make CI green.
  • Do not use one broad loopback helper for both peer addresses and request authority.
  • Do not consider a hosts line exact until every alias token before # is compared.
