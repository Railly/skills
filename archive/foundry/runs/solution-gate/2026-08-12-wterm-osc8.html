Decision

Graft: Fable's ownership, Gemini's separate identity concept, corrected by probes

Each core owns parsing and cell membership. Adapters flatten private state into optional linkUri, explicit linkId, and an opaque linkKey. The renderer never resolves private numeric IDs.
