**Verdict: `needs-human`. Everything was tried logged out, so we can't yet say whether seat selection is reachable.** We tried eight techniques: an automated "Comprar" click with network monitoring, a DOM grep for seat|butaca|asiento, a bundle grep and a React fiber walk. None produced a seat map or a seat endpoint. But all of them ran anonymously: `/api/auth/session` returned `{}`. The config also points to a gate we never passed. `/api/config` sets `"BuyAsGuest":false` and also sets `"SeatMapEnabled":true` and `"MaxTicketsPerOrder":10`. The bundle has a `seat-map` chunk that was never requested, which fits a component that only loads after login. It doesn't mean the feature is missing. The likely reading is that "Comprar" does nothing, or quietly redirects, for anonymous users, and that seat selection sits behind NextAuth login (likely a ClubCinemark account, per `/api/auth/providers`). We also never looked at the page after the click (no screenshot or page text), so a login modal or redirect that our network and DOM checks missed is still possible. **To settle it:** log in with a real ClubCinemark account, click "Comprar" on a session, and record a screenshot plus the network log. Expect a lazy load of the `seat-map` chunk and a request for seat availability. Until that's done, seat reservation in the CLI should be marked "requires authenticated session, unverified", not "unsupported".
