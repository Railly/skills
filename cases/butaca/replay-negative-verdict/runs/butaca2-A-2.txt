## Purchase / seat selection flow

**Verdict:** Seat selection is not reachable for the CLI through the surface mapped here, and the seat-reservation goal is effectively blocked at this stage. Although `/api/config` advertises `SeatMapEnabled: true`, eight independent probes — network monitoring after "Comprar", DOM grep for `seat|butaca|asiento`, bundle inspection, and a React fiber walk — all failed to surface a seat map, a seat component, or any seat-related endpoint, and the "seat-map" chunk is never requested. Combined with `BuyAsGuest: false`, this indicates the purchase flow is gated behind authentication (NextAuth is present but `/api/auth/session` returns `{}`, i.e. unauthenticated) and very likely hands off to an external or lazily-loaded ticketing/checkout module that only mounts after login and session selection. The read-side endpoints (`/api/movies`, `/api/showtimes`, `/api/cinemas`, `/api/formats`) are clean and public, so a **listings-only** CLI is fully viable now; seat reservation, however, should be treated as unverified and out of scope until an authenticated session is captured and the post-login network traffic is re-examined, since the seat API surface could not be observed without credentials. Recommend scoping v1 to showtimes and treating reservation as a follow-up spike contingent on authenticated recon.
