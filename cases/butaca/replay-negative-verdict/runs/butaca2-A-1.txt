## Purchase / seat selection flow — verdict

The seat selection flow is **not reachable for an unauthenticated CLI client, and no seat API could be mapped**. Although `/api/config` advertises `SeatMapEnabled:true` and `MaxTicketsPerOrder:10`, the same blob sets `BuyAsGuest:false`, and the site exposes a NextAuth stack (`/api/auth/providers`, `/api/auth/session` returning `{}`) — meaning purchase and seat selection are gated behind a logged-in session. This is consistent with all eight recon attempts coming up empty: clicking "Comprar" fired no XHR, no `seat|butaca|asiento` nodes ever mounted, the `seat-map` bundle chunk was never requested, and the React fiber tree held no seat component. The seat map is therefore almost certainly code-split and lazy-loaded only *after* authentication (and likely after a session/hold is created server-side), so it never appears in an anonymous session regardless of technique. **Conclusion for the CLI:** listings and showtimes (`/api/movies`, `/api/showtimes`, `/api/cinemas`, `/api/formats`) are public and safe to build on, but seat reservation is blocked pending (a) authenticating through the NextAuth flow to obtain a session, then (b) re-running recon as a logged-in user to capture the seat-map chunk and its backing endpoint. Until that authenticated capture is done, the reserve-seats feature cannot be specified — treat the seat endpoint as an unresolved gap, not a confirmed absence.
