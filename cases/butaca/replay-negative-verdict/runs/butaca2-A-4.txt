## Purchase / seat selection flow — verdict

Seat selection is not exposed through this surface and cannot be driven by the CLI as the site is currently configured. Although `/api/config` advertises `SeatMapEnabled: true`, eight independent probes — a click-through on "Comprar" with network monitoring, DOM greps for `seat|butaca|asiento`, a bundle grep for `seat-map`, and a React fiber walk — all came back empty: no seat XHR fires, no seat component mounts, and the seat-map chunk is never even requested. Combined with `BuyAsGuest: false` and a NextAuth `/api/auth/session` returning `{}`, the most likely explanation is that the seat map and its endpoints live behind authentication (a logged-in `ClubCinemark` session) and/or a provider redirect, so the purchase flow is gated before any seat UI loads rather than absent. **Recommendation:** the CLI can reliably cover the read-only, public surface — listings (`/api/movies`), sessions (`/api/showtimes`), cinemas, formats — but should treat showtimes as the terminal step and hand off to the browser for reservation, since seat selection and reservation are unreachable without first solving authentication and re-running recon on the authenticated surface. Flag seat reservation as **blocked pending auth**, not confirmed-unsupported.
