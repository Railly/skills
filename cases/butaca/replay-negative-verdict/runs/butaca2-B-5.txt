## Purchase / seat selection flow

**Verdict: `needs-human`. The flow was gated behind login and was never observed. This is not evidence that it doesn't exist.** All eight attempts ran anonymously. `/api/auth/session` returned `{}`, and `/api/config` sets `"BuyAsGuest":false` while also setting `"SeatMapEnabled":true` and `"MaxTicketsPerOrder":10`. So the site says seat selection is on but closed to guests. That fits everything we saw. Clicking "Comprar" with no session fired no XHR, most likely because the click is blocked on the client or turned into a login prompt. No seat DOM or React component mounted. The `seat-map` chunk is lazy-loaded and was never requested because we never got past the auth check. The eight techniques aren't independent: they share one condition (an anonymous session) and all measure the same thing, which is that the gate held. They say nothing about what sits behind it. We also didn't check what the user actually saw after clicking, such as a login modal, a redirect to ClubCinemark, or a toast. A screenshot or page text there would settle the gate question directly. **To resolve:** log in with a ClubCinemark account through the provider in `/api/auth/providers`, confirm `/api/auth/session` is non-empty, and click "Comprar" on a session again. Then capture three things: the screenshot after the click, the XHRs that fire (expect a seat-map/session-layout request and a chunk fetch for `seat-map`), and any hold or reserve endpoint along with its auth headers. Until that's done, the CLI's reserve-seats feature can't be scoped. Showtime listing is fine to build now on the public `/api/movies`, `/api/cinemas` and `/api/showtimes`.
