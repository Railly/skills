## Purchase / seat selection flow

**Verdict:** Seat selection is not reachable from the public surface and should be treated as out of scope for the CLI's reservation feature. Although `/api/config` advertises `"SeatMapEnabled":true` and a `MaxTicketsPerOrder` of 10, eight independent probes — network monitoring after "Comprar", DOM/fiber inspection, and bundle grepping — produced no seat map, no seat component, and no seat-related endpoint; the `seat-map` chunk exists but is never requested in the observed flow. Combined with `"BuyAsGuest":false` and a NextAuth session that returns `{}`, this strongly indicates the entire purchase and seat-selection path is gated behind authenticated/loyalty (`ClubCinemark`) state and likely lives on a separate checkout origin or lazy-loaded route that only mounts post-login. The CLI can reliably consume the public read endpoints (`/api/movies`, `/api/showtimes`, `/api/cinemas`, `/api/formats`) to list showtimes, but reserving seats cannot be built against anything captured here: it would require authenticating through NextAuth first and re-running recon on the post-login session to discover the seat/hold/checkout endpoints, which are not exposed to an anonymous client. Recommend shipping listings now and flagging seat reservation as blocked pending authenticated-session recon.
