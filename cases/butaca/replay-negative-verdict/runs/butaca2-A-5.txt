## Purchase / seat selection flow — verdict

The seat-selection flow is **not reachable for recon in its current state, and almost certainly gated behind authentication** rather than absent. The config blob advertises `SeatMapEnabled:true` with `MaxTicketsPerOrder:10`, so a seat map demonstrably exists in the product — but all eight independent probes (XHR monitoring, DOM grep for `seat|butaca|asiento`, bundle grep for `seat-map`, and a React fiber walk) ran against an **unauthenticated** context: `/api/auth/session` returns `{}`, `/api/auth/providers` exposes NextAuth, and `BuyAsGuest:false` means there is no guest path at all. The telling signal is that the `seat-map` chunk exists in the bundle manifest but is **never requested** and no seat component ever mounts after clicking "Comprar" — consistent with a route/component that is code-split and only loaded once a logged-in session (ClubCinemark via NextAuth) is established and a session is selected. **Implication for the CLI:** showtime listing is clean and fully feasible on the public, unauthenticated endpoints (`/api/movies`, `/api/showtimes`, `/api/cinemas`, `/api/formats`), but seat reservation cannot be specified from this recon — the seat endpoint, its request/response shape, and the hold/lock semantics all live behind login and were never observed. Reserving seats will require a second recon pass performed **with an authenticated NextAuth session** to capture the seat-map XHR and the reservation call before any reserve feature can be scoped.
