**Purchase / seat selection flow** — Not reachable anonymously; the flow could not be exercised and a seat-reservation CLI is not validated. The site gates purchasing behind authentication: `/api/config` returns `"BuyAsGuest": false` (with `"SeatMapEnabled": true`), and no account was used at any point in this recon. Every attempt to advance past a showtime confirms the gate rather than a bug — clicking "Comprar" fired no new XHR (attempts 1–3), the post-click DOM contained zero `seat|butaca|asiento` matches (4–5), the only seat-related code ("seat-map") lives in a JS chunk that never loaded (6–7), and no seat component was ever mounted in the React tree (8). These are consistent symptoms of a guest being stopped before the seat-map step, not of a broken or hidden endpoint. Consequently the actual reservation calls (seat map fetch, hold/lock, checkout) were never observed, so their endpoints, payloads, and any hold timers remain unknown. Validating a reservation CLI requires repeating this flow with a real authenticated session (respecting the site's terms and any rate/account constraints), after which the seat-map and hold/checkout traffic can be captured and documented.
