Clicking "Comprar" without a session was therefore probably refused or redirected on the client. Other evidence points the same way: the same config reports `"SeatMapEnabled": true`, the bundle contains a "seat-map" chunk that loads on demand, and the site exposes `/api/auth/providers` and `/api/auth/session`. We have no seat-map endpoint, no request or response shape, no hold or lock step and no checkout handoff, and the eight anonymous attempts don't count as evidence either way. Until that is done, seat reservation is an open gap and the CLI can't be scoped for it.
